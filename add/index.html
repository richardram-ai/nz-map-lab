From this phone

    How to use this form

    1. Where is it? Stand near the thing and let the pin settle. Drag the pin, or tap the map, if it isn't quite right. The dashed box is the area your group works in most.
    2. No signal where you're going? Before you leave, with Wi‑Fi, tap Save the map for offline use under the map. It saves the aerial photos of the dashed box and the photo helper, so both work without signal. Records you save offline send when you're back in range.
    3. An area rather than a spot? Tap Walk a boundary, then Start walking and walk around its edge. Tap Corner here at each corner, Pause to stop recording while you go round an obstacle, and Finish the area when you're back near the start. It works without signal. Phone GPS is usually within 5–10 m, so small areas come out rough; check the shape before saving.
    4. What are you recording? Pick the job: a sighting, a planting, a trap check and so on.
    5. Details. Tap the options that fit. Long lists have a search box, and the names you use most move to the top. Anything not marked optional is needed.
    6. Photos are optional unless you're not sure what something is. For plants, a close-up of a leaf or flower works best.
    7. Note for anything else worth knowing.
    8. Save record. Check the title shown above the button, then save.

    Not sure what it is?

    • Tap Suggest from a photo under the species list. It shows the most likely species with a percentage; pick one if it matches, or “None of these”.
    • The first time, it downloads about 100 MB once (best on wifi), then works without signal.
    • Or choose Other / not sure and add a photo. The record is marked “needs ID” and someone checks it later. That's fine: an honest “not sure” is more useful than a guess.

    Good to know

    • No signal? Save anyway. Records wait on this phone and send themselves when you're back in range. They're listed under “From this phone”.
    • Add the form to your home screen (Share, then Add to Home Screen) so it opens like an app, even offline.
    • Your name, the time and the place are shown with your records on the group's map.
    • Made a mistake? Tell your coordinator, who can correct or remove a record.
