Ruahine Map Lab

Walking access across Ruahine Forest Park (95,000 ha), and a detailed block at the end of Kashmir Road, Ashley Clinton, where wilding pines are being cleared. The block has aerial photos from 1980 to 2025–26 and 2023–24 LiDAR. Draw the work area and the parts that are cleared, not yet cleared or growing back; field records come from the phone form.

    Open questions

    • The real work areaThe Kashmir Road block is a provisional box round the road end. Once the work area is drawn, the block (and the offline download) can be fitted to it.
    • Herenga ā Nuku licenceThe Herenga ā Nuku site states CC BY 4.0, but its track and access‑land services carry no licence text. Confirm before the map is made public.
    • Two regions, two sets of aerialsEach aerial year joins the Hawke's Bay survey (east) with the nearest Manawatū‑Whanganui survey (west), over the 2025–26 satellite. The years don't always match: west is 2015–16 for “2014” and 2024 for “2025”, and there's no western survey for 2019.
    • LiDAR predates the harvestBoth Hawke's Bay LiDAR flights (2020–21 and 2023–24) were before DOC's 2024–25 harvest, so LiDAR can't show the clearance yet. The 2025–26 aerial does.
    • Plant listThe phone form's plant list is a starter list for the eastern Ruahine foothills. Replace it with the group's list and the names they use.
    • Offline in the fieldOpen the phone form with signal, tap “Save the map for offline use”, and let the photo helper download once. After that the form, map tiles for the block and species suggestions work without signal.
